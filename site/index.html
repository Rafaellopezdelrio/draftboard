Draft better. Learn from your own games.

Draftboard is a free, open-source desktop app for League of Legends. It suggests picks and bans in champion select, gives light tips in game, and explains each of your games afterwards in plain words.

View the project on GitHub What data it uses

What it does

Champion select advisor

Picks and bans from your own pool, mastery, matchups and team composition (engage, frontline, damage split).

Team and lane info

Rank and recent form of your teammates. Enemy info only from the loading screen, when names are visible to everyone.

Runes, spells and items

Optional one-click import into the client after you lock in. It can be turned off.

In-game tips

Optional overlay and voice tips, built only on information already on your screen.

Post-game coach

Your laning and game summary, with an AI-written plan that only explains numbers from your own match.

Tier lists by rank

Lane tier lists for your rank, with a clear rule: an S tier champion must win at least 51% of its games.

Fair play

  • Official APIs only: the Riot Games API, the League Client API and the Live Client Data API.
  • No memory reading, no automation of gameplay, no enemy cooldown or ultimate timers.
  • Never reveals hidden players: enemy info starts at the loading screen.
  • No MMR or ELO calculators, no shaming of other players.
  • The Riot API key never ships in the app: it stays on our server, which only forwards the app's own requests.

Your data

  • Your games and settings stay on your PC. Our server keeps only a short cache (up to 24 h).
  • AI review: a summary of your own match is sent to the AI provider (Groq) to write the advice. It is not sold or used for anything else.
  • Optional and off by default: anonymous error reports and a daily usage count, with a random install id. No Riot ID, no IP address.
  • Suggestions and bug reports are sent only when you write them.

Status

Draftboard is in a small closed beta with a few players. It is free, has no ads and nothing to buy. The source code is MIT licensed. Questions or feedback: GitHub issues.
